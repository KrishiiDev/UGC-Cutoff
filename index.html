UGC Course Finder

Find university courses you can enter with your Z-score

Enter your details above and press “Find courses”.

Cutoffs are the minimum Z-scores of students selected in the UGC admission for that year (A/L 2023 → 2023/2024, A/L 2024 → 2024/2025, A/L 2025 → 2025/2026). They show last year’s pattern, not a guarantee. Stream matching follows the UGC Student Handbook 2025/2026 (Section 2.1); subject requirements still apply — confirm them in the Handbook. Source: University Grants Commission, Sri Lanka.
